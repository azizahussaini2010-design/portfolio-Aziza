01 / ABOUT ME

Student today.
Building toward tomorrow.

I believe learning can be a way to open doors.

I'm Aziza Hussaini, a 15-year-old student from Afghanistan who loves learning, making things, and finding ways to support others.

I'm currently studying in Grade 9 through Living Wisdom International Online High School. My interests bring together creativity and problem-solving: I paint and draw, explore front-end development, and enjoy learning languages and new perspectives.

Art helps me express ideas that are sometimes difficult to put into words. Technology gives me another way to build and share ideas. And volunteering has taught me that leadership doesn't have to begin with a title or a big budget—it can begin by noticing what people need and offering what you can.

Curiosity first, always. — Aziza
